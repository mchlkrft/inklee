## Q7 — withdrawal notices sent to Inklee are received when Inklee receives them

**The condition** (`docs/legal/counsel-handoff-2026-08-02.md` Q7, verbatim):

> Addressing the form to the **artist as seller** is correct — the withdrawal
> is from the contract with the artist — and naming Inklee as an alternative
> recipient is permitted and useful, provided the page and the Terms state that
> a withdrawal received by Inklee is forwarded to the artist **without delay**
> and counts as received when Inklee receives it (the buyer must not lose days
> in forwarding).

**What is built.** The naming, and the buyer-facing half of the condition.
`withdrawalForwardingNotice()` (`packages/shared/src/consumer-disclosures.ts`)
carries the rule and is rendered on the model-form page
(`apps/web/src/app/[slug]/shop/withdrawal-form/page.tsx`, which the checkout
page links as `withdrawalFormHref`) and reproduced inside the durable receipt
via `modelWithdrawalFormText()`. The form's "To:" line names the artist and
Inklee (`modelWithdrawalFormLines`).

**What is missing, and why it matters.** The **Terms** half. Counsel's
permission was conditioned on both, so the shipped state names Inklee without
the condition that permits it. The substance is not decorative: the artist is
the seller, the 14-day clock runs against the artist, and the clause is what
binds the artist to the Inklee-receipt date rather than the date the notice
reaches them.

**Proposed wording for the new Section 13** (drafted here; not counsel-approved
wording, since counsel specified the rule and not a sentence):

> **Withdrawal notices sent to Inklee.** A buyer may send a withdrawal or
> return notice either to you or to Inklee. A notice that reaches Inklee counts
> as received on the day Inklee receives it, and Inklee passes it to you
> without delay. When you work out whether a buyer withdrew in time, you must
> use the date Inklee received the notice, not the date it reached you.

Deliberately written without em-dashes, per the founder copy rule on
user-visible strings. The same rule is why the shipped `returnRightNotice()`
renders counsel's C1.2 sentence with commas where counsel's own draft above
uses em-dashes. Keep it that way through the edit.

**Placement note for counsel.** The wording above addresses the ARTIST, because
`terms.md` is an artist-facing document throughout (Section 12 already opens
"You are the seller / merchant of record"). That is not the whole story here:
the goods receipt reproduces the current Terms snapshot in full on the durable
medium (counsel Q6(b); `buildOrderReceiptBody`'s `termsSection`, sourced from
the versioned snapshot in `goods-checkout.ts`), so a BUYER receives this
document too and will read this clause as written to the artist. It still tells
the buyer the rule, and the buyer-facing statement of it already exists in
plainer terms on the model-form page. Worth a look in the confirmation pass in
case counsel wants it phrased for both readers.

## Q12 — deleting an account ends a paid subscription and refunds the remainder

**The instruction** (`docs/legal/counsel-handoff-2026-08-02.md` Q12, verbatim):

> Adopt one rule, disclosed on the confirmation screen and in the Terms:
> **account deletion ends the subscription immediately and refunds the unused
> part of the current period pro rata** (inside the 14-day window it is simply
> processed as a withdrawal, same arithmetic, existing machinery). Rationale:
> period-end semantics leave a paid period attached to a destroyed account;
> silent forfeiture is the unfair-term shape; and at a 3.00 EUR price the refund
> is trivial against the risk and support cost. Confirmation-screen line: "Your
> Inklee Plus subscription ends now. We will refund the unused part of your
> current period." Add the clause to the single C1.9 Terms version — agreed that
> this was the one genuine gap in the prior package.

**What is built.** Both lanes, in `endSubscriptionForAccountDeletion`
(`apps/web/src/lib/server/billing/deletion-refund.ts`, commit `ae9d6c50`):
inside the 14-day window it delegates to the existing withdrawal machinery;
outside it computes `computeAccountDeletionRefund`
(`packages/shared/src/billing.ts`, policy version `deletion-pro-rata-v1`) and
refunds to the original payment method. Counsel's confirmation-screen line is
rendered verbatim on web (`delete-account-section.tsx`) and native
(`apps/mobile/app/account/delete.tsx`), gated on there being an active
subscription.

**PROVENANCE CORRECTION.** The round-4 handoff §1 states that "the implementing
engineer drafted the corresponding Terms clause and reported it", and the task
brief for this edit repeated that and said to reuse the draft verbatim. **No
such draft exists in this repository.** Searched: round-3 and round-4 handoffs,
`plus-build-progress.md`, `plus-build-time-decisions.md`, the whole of `docs/`
and `legal/`, and the history of every doc touched by `ae9d6c50` (that commit
changed no doc other than `web-native-parity.md`). What the engineer actually
produced and reported was the **confirmation-screen** line, which is counsel's
own sentence rendered verbatim, plus the code comments in `deletion-refund.ts`
and `billing.ts` quoting the Q12 answer. **The wording below is therefore
drafted new, here, from counsel's answer and from the behaviour as built. It is
not a verbatim reuse of anything, because there was nothing to reuse.**

**Proposed wording for Section 11 ("Inklee Plus subscription"),** as a bullet
directly after "How to cancel", whose promise it qualifies (that bullet
currently says Plus access "continues until the end of the current paid
period", which is not what deletion does). The cross-reference is written as
"in this section" rather than "above" or "below" on purpose: the "Consumer
withdrawal right" bullet sits further down the same list today, and a
directional reference breaks silently if the bullets are ever reordered.

> **Deleting your account.** Deleting your Inklee account ends your Inklee Plus
> subscription immediately, rather than at the end of the current period. We
> refund the part of the current period you have paid for but not used, in
> proportion to the time remaining, to your original payment method. If you are
> still inside your 14-day withdrawal period, we handle it as a withdrawal
> instead, under the consumer withdrawal right in this section.

**One deliberate departure from counsel's parenthetical, flagged because it is
a money difference.** Counsel wrote that inside the 14-day window a deletion is
"simply processed as a withdrawal, **same arithmetic**, existing machinery".
The first and third are exactly what was built; the middle one is not true of
the code, and the gap favours the consumer. `computeWithdrawalProration`
(`packages/shared/src/billing.ts`) refunds the WHOLE period unless the consumer
expressly requested immediate performance (`fullRefund =
immediatePerformanceRequested !== true`), because without that request no
proportionate deduction is lawful. So a deletion on day three by someone who
never asked for immediate performance is refunded in full, not pro rata. The
proposed clause therefore says "we handle it as a withdrawal instead, under the
consumer withdrawal right above" and stops there, letting the withdrawal
paragraph govern. Saying "the same calculation" in the Terms would promise a
smaller refund than the code actually pays, which is the wrong direction for a
consumer term.

**One sentence beyond what counsel wrote, flagged rather than folded in.** The
implementation deliberately does not block erasure on a failed refund (counsel
§3 of the account-deletion handoff: "erasure is NOT blocked on financial
resolution"), and instead records the obligation in the retained financial
archive as `refundState: "pending"` with the charge id and the amount owed.
`deletion-refund.ts:48-60` flags that counsel never addressed the failure case
and that the opposite reading is defensible. If counsel wants that disclosed,
this is the sentence, to be appended to the bullet above:

> Deleting your account is never held up by a refund. If the refund cannot be
> completed at the time, we keep the limited records needed to pay it and
> complete it afterwards.

**Do not add it without a ruling.** It describes real behaviour, but disclosing
a failure path counsel has not ruled on is the same shape as the §2
carve-out that round 4 had to unwind: an engineering legal determination
arriving in a shipped document.

## Q20 — P2B section for the artist Terms (round-2 Q20, drafted per round-5 §4.1)

**Status: engineering draft, NOT approved wording.** Counsel's round-2 Q20
ruling is complete ("a short P2B section in the artist Terms, landing in the
same C1.9 version"); what follows is the draft that discharges it, written
for the consolidated confirmation pass. It was absent from this package
until 2026-08-03 (round-5 §4.1 records the gap); a C1.9 version without it
has not discharged Q20.

Counsel's ruling, condensed: the P2B Regulation (2019/1150) applies at any
size. Owed now: plain-language Terms; stated grounds and notice for
suspension/termination/restriction of an artist's shop, with a statement of
reasons on suspension; 15 days' minimum notice for Terms changes (folds into
the versioned-Terms process); ranking-parameter disclosure only where
ranking exists; differentiated-treatment disclosure only if Inklee ever
sells its own goods (it does not). The Art. 11 internal complaint-handling
system and Art. 12 mediation duties are exempt for small enterprises, and
the exemption is to be RECORDED, not silently relied on.

**Proposed wording** (new subsection inside the artist-facing part of the
Terms, suggested as "Platform terms for your shop"):

> These Terms are the full agreement for your use of Inklee as a sales
> surface, and we keep them in plain language and available to you at any
> time.
>
> We may restrict, suspend or end your shop or your account only on these
> grounds: a breach of these Terms, content or conduct that is illegal or
> infringes others' rights, fraud or payment abuse, or a legal or regulatory
> requirement. If we restrict or suspend your shop, we will give you a
> statement of reasons on a durable medium. If we end your shop entirely, we
> will give you the statement of reasons at least 30 days before that takes
> effect, unless a legal obligation or a repeated breach requires us to act
> sooner.
>
> We will tell you about changes to these Terms at least 15 days before they
> take effect, and longer where you would need to make technical changes to
> keep selling. You can close your account before a change takes effect if
> you do not accept it.
>
> Your shop lists only your own products. Inklee does not rank your products
> against another artist's at the point of sale, and Inklee does not sell
> goods of its own on the platform. If either of those changes, we will
> disclose how it works.
>
> Inklee is currently a small enterprise under EU Recommendation 2003/361.
> The P2B Regulation's internal complaint-handling and mediation duties do
> not apply to enterprises of that size, and we record that here; if our
> size classification changes, this section will change with it.

**Open points for the confirmation pass (counsel decides, not engineering):**
